Task 1

As a professional and an active member of The Church of Jesus Christ, following the example of Jesus Christ has been fundamental to my life. It has enabled me to make decisions that have helped me enhance both my spiritual and temporal knowledge, allowing me to better serve my home, my workplace, and the Church. The attributes of Jesus Christ—values and principles that have shown me the right path to follow—have been instrumental in this regard; I therefore highlight those that have most helped me move forward steadfastly and abound in good works

Hope

Hope has been a source of encouragement in my life during difficult times, serving as a reminder when I felt overwhelmed and hopeless about my situation. I remember first feeling this discouragement during the pandemic, when we were unable to leave our homes. I felt unable to contribute financially to the household; although I worked as a basic English teacher at an institute, it wasn't enough because I was teaching only by the hour rather than full-time. I had lost hope; however, amidst it all, praying and reflecting on Jesus Christ and what He endured reminded me not to lose faith. I prayed to the Father, asking for help in finding a job aligned with my professional degree in accounting. The answer did not come immediately, but it did arrive in a special, spiritual way: I was selected to start as a teller at Banco Unión, a well-known bank in my city. That experience taught me that Jesus Christ and the Father are there to remind us that faith and hope are essential attributes in both our professional and spiritual lives.

Integrity

Integrity has served as a warning signal during moments when I felt my moral values wavering—times when, acting on impulse, I was tempted to cheat or be dishonest with myself, whether at work or in my personal life. Thanks to Jesus Christ, I remembered how He remained steadfast in His mission and conviction to fulfill the Atonement during His earthly ministry; His integrity has been an example to me of how to resist the adversary's temptations and uphold my moral values. This has been a great blessing in my life, as it gave me the opportunity to build trust with my employers, friends, and family. It also enabled me to act as a peacemaker, fostering calm and harmony wherever I have been. I know it can often be difficult, but through Jesus Christ, I learned that things are never easy and that the rewards of a life of integrity require sacrifice.

Diligence

Diligent work and the pursuit of knowledge through study and experience have instilled in me a genuine commitment to the tasks and assignments I received during my university years and my professional career. I view this as a Christian attribute taught by Jesus Christ; He is a supreme example of diligence, having attended to His Father’s business and built up the Church on earth from a young age. This example inspired in me a desire to perform tasks diligently, without expecting anything in return other than the knowledge that—provided my work was good and characterized by integrity—blessings would arrive in due time. That is precisely what happened; I witnessed great miracles in my personal and spiritual life as well as in my professional career. Consequently, this attribute has become a cornerstone of my personal growth and character, helping me be a better son and disciple of the Lord. Whenever I faltered—feeling sluggish or slow to complete my assignments—I would ask myself what the Savior would have done in my place. That thought would spur me to action, motivating me to resume my work with diligence, confident that blessings and rewards would follow at the right time.

Ethical Dilema

Reflecting on the ethical dilemma I cited as an example, I believe that plagiarism—specifically reusing third-party code without giving proper credit—violates moral and ethical values. In the field of computer science, one must exercise great care regarding code reuse; failing to attribute credit can lead to authorship disputes and is fundamentally dishonest. Based on the principles I have lived by; I know this would be detrimental; it would damage both one's reputation and self-image. Therefore, upholding principles of honesty and integrity is crucial when reusing code created by others.

Regarding the workplace, I believe that teamwork—whether as a developer, programmer, or systems technician—is essential for the team's success. When I need assistance with code or need to reuse existing code, I must be diligent about giving proper credit and being honest with my colleagues. Instead of claiming credit for work that isn't mine, I should prioritize a team-oriented mindset and open communication to prevent future issues. A common challenge in the workplace is the tendency to compare ourselves to others and undervalue our own potential. However, if we seek to expand our knowledge and improve in the tasks assigned by our employers, meeting high expectations becomes a matter of honest, diligent work. Possessing ethical and moral values is vital for facing these challenges; honesty—and avoiding the plagiarism of others' work—provides peace of mind that we are doing the right thing, while also fostering trust and a positive professional image among colleagues.

Reflection Activity: Attend the Temple

I was unable to attend the temple this week; the primary reason was the distance—I have to travel eight hours to reach the Cochabamba Bolivia Temple. Additionally, the Church’s General Conference took place. My reflection this week centers on the talks given during that event; one in particular—which focused on the temple—really caught my attention. Elder Gerrit W. Gong spoke about the importance of temple attendance and appreciating the blessings temples bring to our lives, noting how more temples are being dedicated closer to home. He mentioned an upcoming dedication, which reminded me that a temple has been announced for my own city, La Paz—the groundbreaking has already taken place. This made me reflect on how, just as we feel great excitement and enthusiasm at the beginning of our temple experience, that feeling can fade over time.

I have experienced this personally in my professional growth; I have often felt discouraged by the sheer amount there is to learn. However, I believe the things I learned in the Church helped me persevere. It is similar to starting a career: at first, everything seems clear, but over time, things can become less distinct. Yet, the truth is that if we remain diligent and persistent, we can gain greater clarity regarding our goals. In this way, the things I have learned—my religious beliefs—have connected with my academic and professional aspirations. The temple has mirrored my own life experiences regarding the sacrifices required to achieve my goals; I know that the blessings of service and sacrifice are sacred principles that have aided me in my pursuit of happiness.
