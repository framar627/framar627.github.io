Core Principles & Experiences

Honesty & Integrity

Foundational Workplace Value

Honest actions bring security and peace of mind. During my experience as a bank teller, receiving counterfeit bills tested this resolve. Remembering my bishop's advice, I refused to pass the burden to others, stopping a potential chain of dishonest transactions.

Work & Responsibility

Accountability in Operations

Managing high-volume customer accounts and strict transaction targets taught me diligence. Meeting these benchmarks earned the trust of my bank supervisors, paving the way for promotions into supervisory and document review roles.

Sacrifice & Dedication

Academic & Personal Growth

Finishing high school, graduating seminary, working night shifts at an oven to save for university, and taking preparatory courses demanded immense sacrifice—leading to top academic success and an accounting degree.

Spiritual Growth & Reflection

Temple Reflection & Service

A trip to the Cochabamba Bolivia Temple reinforced the truth that serving others aligns us with God's work. Supporting family members through hardships directly translated into greater empathy and leadership skills at work, leading to opportunities to step in for my bank supervisor.
